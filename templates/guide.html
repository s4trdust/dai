How Re:Source works

  1. 01Deposit – Place your scrap on the Physical AI scanning station.
  2. 02Identify – AI identifies the material and evaluates its condition.
  3. 03Store – Follow automatic sorting or manual placement instructions.
  4. 04Discover – Search the Digital Material Library for materials.
  5. 05Reuse – Check out what you take: scan the QR label on large and unique items, or pick small scraps from the list.
Scrapyard etiquette
  • Only deposit accepted materials.
  • Return unused materials to their designated locations.
  • Check out everything you take on the Checkout page.
  • Keep storage areas clean and organised.
  • Ask staff for help with uncertain or unsafe items.
Not accepted
  • Batteries, power banks and damaged electronics: take to the e-waste bin.
  • Furniture, stationery, plushies, textbooks: take to the nearest collection point.
  • Damaged, leaking or contaminated items: stop and ask staff.
  • Unsure what it is? Place it in the manual-check area.
