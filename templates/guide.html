📥 Depositing

  1. Open Register and put the item on the station's scale.
  2. Press "Get weight from scale", then take or choose a photo: the AI fills in the details.
  3. Check the details. If the AI is under 95% sure, tick the box to confirm them.
  4. Small pieces: the funnel turns to their section. Anything else: follow the placement shown and stick on the label.

🛒 Taking an item

  1. Search the Library or ask the AI Matcher first: no wasted trip.
  2. Large / unique items: scan the QR label on the Checkout page, then confirm.
  3. Small scraps: pick them from the list on the Checkout page.

🤝 Etiquette

  • Leave the shelf tidier than you found it.
  • Don't peel labels off items you're not taking.
  • Return unused pieces through the deposit station, not the floor.
  • Ask staff for help with uncertain or unsafe items.

🚫 Not accepted here

  • Batteries & power banks: e-waste bin opposite Insta Chef.
  • Damaged electronics: school e-waste collection point.
  • Furniture, stationery, plushies, textbooks: their own collection points.
  • Leaking / contaminated items: stop and find the staff in charge.
  • Not sure? The manual area.

The AI warns you on the Register page if it spots one of these.
